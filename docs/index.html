Questions

Is my data safe?

Yes. Everything stays on your local Wi-Fi, encrypted with TLS. Each phone is individually paired and authenticated — your Mac only obeys devices you approved. Camera and microphone are processed entirely on the iPhone and never transmitted. We collect nothing. See the privacy policy.

Why isn't AirBridge in the Mac App Store?

Apps that control the mouse and keyboard need the Accessibility permission, which Mac App Store rules don't allow. AirBridge is signed and notarized by Apple's security check instead — the standard for this category.

What does AirBridge need to work?

macOS 14 or later, and the Accessibility permission (System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility) so it can move the cursor and type on your behalf.

Does it work over the internet?

No — by design. AirPad only works on your local network, which is a security feature: your Mac is never exposed to the internet.
