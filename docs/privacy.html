Privacy Policy

AirPad (iOS) & AirBridge (macOS) — Last updated: July 2026
The short version: we collect nothing. No analytics, no accounts, no servers. Everything happens on your devices and your own Wi-Fi network.

Data collection

AirPad and AirBridge do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. There are no user accounts, no analytics or tracking SDKs, no advertising, and no third-party services. We operate no servers: the apps talk only to each other, directly, on your local network.

Local network

AirPad discovers and connects to AirBridge over your local Wi-Fi using Bonjour. All traffic between your iPhone and your Mac is encrypted (TLS) and stays on your network — nothing is ever sent over the internet. Each device must be explicitly paired and is cryptographically authenticated on every connection.

Camera (Hand Mouse)

The optional Hand Mouse feature uses the front camera to track your hand so you can point and pinch to control your Mac. Video frames are analyzed entirely on-device using Apple's Vision framework. No image, video, or hand data ever leaves your iPhone — only ordinary mouse-movement commands are sent to your Mac.

Microphone & speech (Dictation)

The optional Dictation feature uses the microphone and Apple's speech recognition to convert your voice to text, which you review before it is typed on your Mac. Audio is handled by iOS's speech services under Apple's terms; AirPad itself stores no recordings or transcripts.

Motion sensors (Air Mouse)

The Air Mouse feature reads the phone's gyroscope while you hold the aim pad. Motion data is converted to cursor movement on-device and is not stored.

Clipboard

Clipboard contents are transferred between your iPhone and Mac only when you explicitly tap a clipboard action, over the same encrypted local connection, and are never stored by the apps or sent anywhere else.

Purchases

AirPad Pro is a one-time purchase processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment information.

Security

Connections use TLS encryption with a pre-shared key. Each phone is paired per-Mac with a unique secret stored in the device Keychain, and every session is authenticated with a cryptographic challenge before any command is accepted. You can revoke a device's access at any time from AirBridge ("Forget").

Children

The apps collect no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the update will be posted at this address with a new date. Since the apps collect nothing, changes would only ever be clarifications.
